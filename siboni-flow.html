Ltd.'s books. This debt carries zero (0%) interest and is structured to support SiboniTech's growth trajectory until external funding is secured.

0% — No interest charges at any time

Mohit Prasad may elect to convert Tech Debt (all or part) to equity at fair valuation — by mutual written agreement
Acknowledgement
